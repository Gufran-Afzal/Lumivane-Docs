Pages (About, Contact, FAQ)

Pages hold content that does not change often. The theme has four ready-made page layouts.

Create a page

  1. In Shopify choose Online Store → Pages → Add page.
  2. Type a title, for example About us.
  3. On the right, under Theme template, choose the layout from the table below.
  4. Press Save, then open the page in the theme editor to change its sections.
Template Use it for
page Plain text pages such as Shipping or Terms
page.about Your story: banner, statement, rows, numbers, values and founder
page.contact Contact details and a form, with a short FAQ
page.faq Questions and answers grouped by topic
page.ingredients An ingredients page with the orbit and story rows

Add the pages to your menu

Add each page to the Main menu or Footer menu in Content → Menus so visitors can find it.
