The product page

Where shoppers decide to buy. Here is what each part does and how to tune it.

What is on the page

  • Gallery on the left: swipe or use arrows. The small pictures below jump to a photo. Click a photo to zoom.
  • Title, price and rating on the right, then the variant picker (swatches or buttons), quantity and the Add to cart button.
  • Extra blocks such as benefits, a shade guide and an "ask an expert" box.
  • Sections underneath: product story, before and after, reviews and related products.

Settings in plain words

Open Products → Default product in the editor and click Product in the left column.

Setting What it does
Desktop gallery Editorial grid shows photos in a grid. Slider with thumbnails shows one large photo and small pictures underneath or at the side.
Image fit Fill crops the photo to fill the frame. Fit shows the whole photo.
Show the selected variant image first When a shopper picks a shade, show that shade's photo first.
Show sticky add-to-cart bar A small bar that stays at the bottom while scrolling, so the buy button is always near.
Photo shape Portrait (4:5), Square, Landscape, or Match the photo to use each photo's own shape.
Media width How wide the gallery is compared with the text.
Show breadcrumbs A small trail like Home › Skincare › Barrier Serum.

Variant picker

Click the Variant picker block. Style can be Buttons, Dropdown or Variant images. Colour and shade options show as swatches when every value has a colour. See Colour swatches.

Bundles and sets

The template product.ritual-set is made for a set of products. In Shopify open the product and choose it under Theme template.

"Complete the routine" bundle without picking products

In the Bundle block you can choose up to three products. If you leave them empty, the theme shows Shopify's own complementary product suggestions instead. Set those up with the free Shopify Search & Discovery app (Recommendations → Complementary products). When there are no suggestions the bundle hides itself, so there is never an empty box.

Sticky bar variant picker

On small screens the sticky add-to-cart bar has a Variant dropdown, so shoppers can change size or shade without scrolling back up. It stays in sync with the main picker.
