Build your home page

The home page is made of sections. This page shows how to change them and which ones to keep.

How to edit a section

  1. In the editor choose Home page from the page dropdown.
  2. Click a section name in the left column. Its settings open.
  3. Click a block (for example a slide) to edit its text and image.
  4. Drag the dots to reorder, or use the eye icon to hide a section without deleting it.
  5. To add a section press Add section and choose from the list.

The sections on the home page, top to bottom

Section What it is What you change
Hero: glow slider The big first screen with up to several slides, a product picture and floating details Headline, text, button, 4 images per slide
Scrolling marquee A moving line of short claims The words, for example "Vegan · Cruelty free"
Category chips Round pictures that link to concerns or categories Picture and link for each chip
Featured collection Product tabs such as Skincare, Makeup, Body The collection behind each tab
Manifesto A big statement with two photos The statement and photos
Ingredient orbit Ingredient pictures circling a product One picture and short text per ingredient
Ritual steps (pinned) A step-by-step routine Title, text and picture for each step, and the products
Featured product One product with its options and buy button Choose the product
Shade finder A helper that matches a shade The shades, colours and linked products
Cinematic banner A full-screen image that grows as you scroll Image, phone image, headline, button
Stacking cards Cards that stack as you scroll Three or more cards with image and text
Before and after A slider that compares two pictures Both pictures and the result statistics
Story rows Alternating picture and text rows Pictures and text
Collection list A list of concerns, each linking to a collection The collections and pictures
Shoppable image A photo with clickable product pins The photo and the products pinned
Horizontal scroll gallery A moving gallery of pictures and quotes Pictures, quotes, a product
Review wall Customer reviews The names, ratings and text
Countdown A sale timer The end date and the offer
Press quotes Quotes from publications The names and quotes
FAQ Questions and answers The questions
Blog posts Your latest articles Choose the blog

Use real quotes only

The Press quotes and Review wall sections come with sample text. Replace it with real quotes you have permission to use, or remove the section. Never show another brand's name as if they endorsed you.

Good order for a first home page

  1. Hero, 2. Marquee, 3. Category chips, 4. Bestsellers, 5. Ritual steps, 6. Reviews, 7. Newsletter. Add the rest slowly. A calm page converts better than a crowded one.

The hero slider in detail

Each Slide has five pictures and some text.

Field in the editor What to upload
Background image A soft empty scene with space on the left for the headline
Mobile background A tall version of the background for phones
Foreground layer (optional) A soft glow or texture on top
Product image A cut-out of your product with a transparent background
Image (in the floating bubble group) A close-up of one key ingredient, cropped into a circle
