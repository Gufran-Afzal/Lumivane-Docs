Troubleshooting and FAQ

Find the problem you see, then try the fix.

My store looks empty or shows grey shapes

The theme ships with sample content, and photos do not transfer when you install. Open each section in the editor and add your own images and text. See Build your home page.

Shades or colours show as text, not circles

At least one value has no colour. Add every value to Theme settings → Swatches → Swatch colors. Full steps are in Colour swatches.

The cart drawer does not open

Open Theme settings → Cart and check that After adding to cart is set to Open cart drawer. If you installed a cart app, switch it off to test.

Filters do not show on my collection

Filters come from Shopify's free Search & Discovery app. Add filters there, then check Enable filtering in the collection's Product grid section.

Product cards have no colour dots

Turn on Theme settings → Product tiles → Show color swatches. The product also needs an option named Color, Shade, Tone or Colour, and every value needs a colour.

A section is missing on a page

Click the eye icon next to the section in the editor. It may be hidden. Also check that you are editing the right page in the dropdown at the top.

The page jumps or animates too much

Open Theme settings → Motion and choose Calm, or turn Enable scroll animations off.

My text is hard to read on an image

Change the Text color or Tone of that section, or choose a calmer, darker image.

I changed something and want to undo it

Press Undo at the top of the editor. Use Actions → Duplicate to keep a backup before big changes.

Can I use apps?

Yes. Add app blocks where a section offers Add block → Apps. Too many apps slow the store, so add only what you need.

Can I change the theme code?

Please duplicate the theme first. Custom code changes are not covered by support. For custom work, hire a Shopify Partner.

How do I update the theme?

Duplicate your live theme, add the new version in the theme library, then copy your settings across. Test the duplicate before publishing.
