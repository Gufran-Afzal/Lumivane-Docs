Welcome

Lumivane is a Shopify theme for beauty and skincare shops. This guide takes you from an empty store to a finished shop, one small step at a time. You do not need to know how to code.

The three presets

A preset is a ready-made look. You can switch between them in two clicks and your products and text stay the same.

LumivaneWarm, soft and light. Round pill buttons. Good for skincare and everyday beauty.
NightbloomDark and rich with gold touches and square buttons. Good for luxury and fragrance.
FernmistSage greens and soft shapes. Good for botanical and wellness brands.

What you get

  • A home page built from sections: hero slider, product tabs, ingredient circle, routine steps, shade finder, before and after slider, reviews, blog and more.
  • Colour swatches on product pages and product cards.
  • Quick buy on product cards and a cart drawer that slides in.
  • Filtering, sorting and page numbers on collection pages.
  • Instant search that shows results while you type.
  • Animations that you can turn off. They also switch off for visitors who ask their device for less motion.

How to use this guide

Each page starts with what you will do, then numbered steps, then tips. Words in bold are things you click or type. Words like section and preset are explained in Before you start.
