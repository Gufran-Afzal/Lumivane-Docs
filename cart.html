Cart and checkout

What happens when someone presses Add to cart, and how to encourage bigger orders.

Open Theme settings (paintbrush) → Cart.

Setting What it does
After adding to cart Open cart drawer slides the cart in from the side. Go to cart page takes the shopper to the full cart.
Enable order note Lets shoppers write a note for you, for example for a gift.
Free shipping goal Type an amount such as 75. A progress bar tells shoppers how close they are. Leave it empty to hide the bar.
Show recommendations in cart drawer Suggests products the shopper may add.
Cart drawer tone The colour scheme of the drawer.
Show discount code field Shows the box where shoppers type a code.
Show trust message A short reassuring line near the checkout button.

Note

Checkout is controlled by Shopify, not the theme. Change checkout colours and logo in Settings → Checkout and customer accounts → Customize.

Quick buy on product cards

When Enable quick buy is on (Theme settings → Product tiles), each product card shows a button. It opens a small window where shoppers choose a shade or size and add to cart without leaving the page.
