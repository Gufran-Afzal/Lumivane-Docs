Blog and articles

A blog builds trust and brings visitors from search engines.

Write your first article

  1. In Shopify go to Content → Blog posts → Add blog post.
  2. Type a title and write the article. Add a picture of 1600 × 1000 pixels as the featured image.
  3. Choose the blog (the theme's journal uses a blog called Journal, or the one you pick).
  4. Press Save.

Show posts on the home page

Open the home page in the editor and add the Blog posts section. Choose the blog and how many articles to show.

Ideas for articles

How to build an evening routine, what an ingredient does, how to choose your shade, how to read an ingredient list, and answers to the questions customers ask most.
