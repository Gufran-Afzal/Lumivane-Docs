Before you start

Five minutes of reading now saves hours later. These are the only words and ideas you need.

What you need

  • A Shopify store on any paid plan, or a development store for testing.
  • A few product photos. Image sizes tells you what works best.
  • A computer. The theme editor works on phones, but a computer is much easier.

Words used in this guide

Word What it means
Theme The design of your whole store. You are installing one called Lumivane.
Theme editor The Shopify screen where you change the design. Open it from Online Store → Themes → Customize.
Preset A ready-made look of the theme: Lumivane, Nightbloom or Fernmist.
Template The layout of one kind of page: home, product, collection, blog post and so on.
Section A horizontal band on a page, such as a hero image or a list of reviews. You can add, remove and drag sections up and down.
Block A small item inside a section, such as one slide or one question in an FAQ.
Collection A group of products, such as Skincare or Sale.
Variant One version of a product, such as a shade or a size.
Option A choice shoppers make, such as Shade or Size. Each option has several values, such as Honey or 30 ml.
Swatch A small coloured circle that stands for a colour or shade.
Metafield Extra information you can attach to a product. You will rarely need this.

How the theme editor works

  1. Open it. In Shopify choose Online Store → Themes, then press Customize next to your theme.
  2. Pick a page at the top. The dropdown in the middle of the top bar switches between Home, Products, Collections, Blog and more.
  3. Use the left column. It lists the sections of the page. Click one to see its settings. Drag the dots on the left of a section to move it.
  4. Use the paintbrush icon for the whole store. This is Theme settings: colours, fonts, buttons, swatches and cart.
  5. Save. Press Save at the top right. Nothing is visible to shoppers until the theme is published.

You cannot break anything

Changes in the editor only go live when you press Save on the published theme. If you make a mistake, press the back arrow at the top or use Undo. For bigger experiments, duplicate the theme first with Actions → Duplicate.
