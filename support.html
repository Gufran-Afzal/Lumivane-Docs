Get support

We read every message and reply within two business days.

Before you write

  1. Search this guide. Most questions are answered in Troubleshooting.
  2. Note the page address where the problem happens.
  3. Take a screenshot or a short screen recording.

Send a request

What support covers

  • Questions about how the theme's settings work
  • Bugs in the theme

Custom design changes, adding new features and setting up third-party apps are not covered. A Shopify Partner can help with those.

Keep your store safe

Never send your password in a request. If we need access, we will ask you to send a collaborator request from your Shopify admin.
