Image sizes

Good images make the biggest difference to how your shop looks. Use this page as a cheat sheet.

The simple rules

  • Save photos as JPG or WebP. Use PNG only for cut-outs that need a transparent background.
  • Keep each file under 500 KB. Use a free tool such as squoosh.app to shrink it.
  • Upload images bigger than the size below, never smaller. Shopify makes smaller copies for phones for you.
  • Keep the important part of the photo in the middle. Edges are cropped on phones.

Alt text

Alt text is a short description of the image for people who use screen readers. In the editor, click any image and press Edit alt text. Describe what is in the picture, for example "Frosted glass serum bottle on a pink background". Do not start with "image of".
