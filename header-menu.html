Header and menu

The header is the bar at the top of every page. It holds your logo, menu, search and cart.

Add your logo

  1. In the theme editor open any page and click Header in the left column.
  2. Next to Logo press Select and upload your logo. A PNG or SVG that is about 600 px wide works well.
  3. If the header sits on top of a dark image, also upload Logo for transparent header (usually a white version).
  4. Use Logo width and Logo width on mobile to make it bigger or smaller.

Make the menu

  1. In Shopify go to Content → Menus and open Main menu.
  2. Press Add menu item. Type a name and choose where it links, for example a collection.
  3. To make a dropdown, drag an item slightly to the right under another item. That makes it a child.
  4. To make a wide mega menu, add one more level: children that have their own children.
  5. Press Save menu. Back in the editor, make sure Menu in the Header says Main menu.

Header settings in plain words

Setting What it does
Desktop layout Choose where the logo and menu sit: logo in the middle, logo on the left, or logo above the menu.
Sticky header Always keeps it visible while scrolling. Hide on scroll down shows it again when the shopper scrolls up. Off lets it scroll away.
Float over the first section The header sits on top of the first image instead of above it. Pair it with a light logo.
Text and icons over media Light or dark text for a floating header. Pick the one that is easy to read on your image.
Show country/region selector Needs Shopify Markets. Lets shoppers change country and currency.
Show language selector Shows when your store has more than one language.
Popular products collection The products shown when a shopper opens search and has not typed anything yet.

The announcement bar

The thin bar above the header. Open Announcement bar in the editor, then add or edit Message blocks. Each message can have a link and an icon. Messages rotate, and Rotate every sets how many seconds each one stays.
