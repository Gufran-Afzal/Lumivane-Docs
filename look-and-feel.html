Colours, fonts and style

Change the whole look of your store from one place. Everything here is under the paintbrush icon (Theme settings).

Colours

Open Color tones. There are six colour schemes. A scheme is a set of matching colours for a background, text and buttons.

  • Each section has a Tone (or Color scheme) setting where you choose one of the six.
  • Edit a scheme once and every section that uses it changes.
  • Use the first scheme for most sections, a darker one for contrast, and the accent scheme sparingly.

Check contrast

Text must be easy to read. Light grey text on a light background is hard for many people.

Typography

Setting What it does
Font (display) The font for headings
Size scale Makes all heading or body text bigger or smaller in one move. 100 is the normal size.
Letter spacing Space between letters in headings. Small negative numbers look elegant.
Case Show headings in normal case or all capitals
Color italic heading words with the accent In the editor, select a word in a heading and press italic. It then takes the accent colour.
Small labels and eyebrows Spaced capitals or Sentence case for the tiny text above headings

Layout

Setting What it does
Page width The widest the content can be on large screens
Side padding (desktop) Space on the left and right of the page
Space between sections Extra space between sections. Increase it for a more airy page.
Grid spacing Space between products in a grid
Media corner radius How round the corners of pictures are. 0 is square.

Buttons and fields

Setting What it does
Button shape Pill (fully round), Soft (slightly round) or Square
Button hover The effect when a mouse is over a button: Liquid fill, Lift or Text roll
Field corner radius Roundness of text boxes

Product cards

Open Product tiles.

Setting What it does
Image ratio The shape of the card picture: Natural, Square, Portrait or Tall
Show second image on hover Shows the second product photo when the mouse is over the card
Show vendor Shows the brand name
Show product rating Shows stars. Needs a reviews app that writes the standard Shopify rating.
Show color swatches Shows small colour dots. See Colour swatches
Enable quick buy Adds the quick buy button
Show card background Puts the card on a soft coloured panel
Sale badge tone / Sold out badge tone Colours of the badges
Sale badge Show the word Sale, the percentage saved, or the amount saved

Brand and social

  • Brand has the favicon (the tiny icon in the browser tab, 32 × 32 px), a brand statement and a short description that appear in the footer.
  • Social media takes your profile links. Leave a box empty to hide that icon.
