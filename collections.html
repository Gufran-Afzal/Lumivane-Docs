Collections, filters and pagination

A collection page lists your products. Here is how to organise them and let shoppers filter and browse.

Create a collection

  1. In Shopify choose Products → Collections → Create collection.
  2. Give it a title such as Skincare and an image. The image appears on the collection list page and in the home page sections.
  3. Choose Manual to pick products yourself, or Automated to add products by a rule, for example "tag is skincare".
  4. Save.

Change how a collection page looks

In the theme editor choose Collections → Default collection from the page dropdown. Click Product grid in the left column.

Setting What it does
Products per page How many products appear before the page buttons. 12 or 16 is a good start.
Columns on desktop How many products sit in each row on a computer.
Columns on mobile 1 or 2 per row on a phone.
Enable filtering Shows filters such as price and availability.
Filter layout on desktop Sidebar keeps filters beside the products. Drawer hides them behind a Filter button.
Enable sorting Adds the "Sort by" menu.
Pagination Page numbers, Load more button or Infinite scroll. Page numbers are best for search engines.

Turn on filters

Filters are set up in Shopify, not in the theme.

  1. Install Shopify's free Search & Discovery app from the Shopify App Store.
  2. Open the app and choose Filters.
  3. Add the filters you want, such as Availability, Price, Product type, or a colour filter.
  4. Save. The filters appear on collection pages and on the search page.

Colour filters show as swatches

If you add a colour filter that uses Shopify's swatch colours, the theme shows the colours as small circles in the filter list.

Collection templates

A template decides the layout. This theme has two for collections:

  • collection is the standard layout with a banner, category chips, product grid and FAQ.
  • collection.editorial adds story rows and a text marquee for a magazine feel. To use it, open the collection in Shopify and choose it under Theme template.
