Animations and speed

Motion makes the theme feel alive, but fast loading matters more. Here is how to control both.

Animation settings

Open Theme settings → Motion.

Setting What it does
Enable scroll animations The master switch. Turn it off for a completely still store.
Animation energy Calm, Balanced or Expressive. Calm is gentle and quick. Expressive is bolder.
Image entrance How pictures appear: Curtain wipe, Soft zoom or Fade
Animate headings line by line Headings appear one line at a time
Magnetic buttons Buttons lean slightly towards the mouse on computers
Custom cursor halo A soft circle that follows the mouse on computers
Scroll progress line A thin line at the top that shows how far down the page you are
Page transitions The effect when changing pages: None, Soft fade or Curtain rise

Reduced motion

Visitors who set "Reduce motion" on their phone or computer never see scroll animation, whatever you choose here.

Keep the store fast

  1. Resize and compress every image before uploading. See Image sizes.
  2. Use fewer sections on the home page. Each one adds weight.
  3. Avoid too many apps. Each app can slow the store.
  4. Use a short video (under 10 MB) only if it adds real value.
  5. Test with Google PageSpeed Insights and fix the largest images first.
