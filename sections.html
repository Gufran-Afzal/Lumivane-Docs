Sections A to Z

Every section you can add in the theme editor. Use this page to look up what a section does and what you can change in it.

How to add a section

In the theme editor press Add section on any page and choose from the list. Sections marked header, footer and announcement live in the page header and footer areas.

Header

The top of every page: logo, menu, search and cart.

You can change: Tone, Mobile menu tone, Logo, Logo for transparent header, Logo width, Logo width on mobile, Menu, Desktop layout, Sticky header, Float over the first section, Text and icons over media, Show country/region selector, Show language selector, Popular products collection.

Blocks you can add: Mega menu spotlight.

Announcement bar

Thin bar above the header with rotating short messages.

You can change: Tone, Rotate every, Show social icons on desktop, Show country/language on desktop.

Blocks you can add: Message.

Footer

The bottom of every page: links, newsletter and brand text.

You can change: Tone, Top padding, Show giant wordmark, Wordmark text, Show payment icons, Show policy links, Show country/region selector, Show language selector.

Blocks you can add: Brand, Menu, Text, Email signup.

Hero: glow slider

Big first-screen slider with a product picture, a small round picture and a glow background.

You can change: Accessible name, Height, Text position, Text color, Auto-rotate slides, Change slides every, Pointer parallax on desktop, Scroll hint.

Blocks you can add: Slide.

Scrolling marquee

A moving line of short claims or pictures.

You can change: Tone, Style, Loop duration, Direction, Speed up with scroll, Tilt, Separator, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Text, Image.

Category chips

Round pictures that link to categories or concerns.

You can change: Tone, Label, Show product count, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Chip.

Featured collection

Products from a collection, with optional tabs.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Link label, Link, Layout, Carousel bleeds off screen, Products per tab, Columns on desktop, Columns on mobile, Show view all button, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Collection tab.

Manifesto

A big statement with two photos.

You can change: Tone, Alignment, Eyebrow, Statement, Floating image 1, Floating image 2, Signature, Link label, Link, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Ingredient orbit

Ingredient pictures circling a product, each with a short explanation.

You can change: Tone, Detail card tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Center product image, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Ingredient.

Ritual steps (pinned)

A step-by-step routine that stays on screen while you scroll.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Image ratio, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Step.

Featured product

One product with its options and buy button.

You can change: Tone, Product, Rotating badge text, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: App block, Eyebrow, Title, Price, Text, Variant picker, Buy buttons, View details link.

Shade finder

Helps shoppers match a shade and jump to the product.

You can change: Tone, Result card tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Lightest slider color, Deepest slider color, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Shade.

Cinematic banner

A full-screen image that grows as you scroll, with a headline and button.

You can change: Tone, Image, Mobile image, Background video, Scroll effect, Height, Content position, Image overlay, Text color, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Button label, Button link, Link label, Link.

Stacking cards

Cards that stack on top of each other as you scroll.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Card.

Before and after

Drag a handle to compare a before and after picture, with result numbers.

You can change: Tone, Layout, Image ratio, Before image, Before label, After image, After label, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Footnote, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Result.

Story rows

Alternating picture and text rows.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Image ratio, Start with image on the right, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Row.

Collection list

A list of collections with pictures.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Link label, Link, Layout, Columns on desktop, Image ratio, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Collection.

Shoppable image

A photo with clickable product pins.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Image, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Product pin.

Horizontal scroll gallery

A gallery that moves sideways as you scroll, with pictures, quotes and a product.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Link label, Link, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Image, Product, Quote.

Review wall

A wall of customer reviews.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Average score, Score label, Columns on desktop, Scroll duration, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Review.

Countdown

A sale timer with an offer.

You can change: Tone, Image, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, End date, End time, Time zone offset, Message after it ends, Hide section when it ends, Button label, Button link, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Press quotes

Quotes from publications, with their names.

You can change: Tone, Rotate every, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Press quote.

FAQ

Questions and answers that open and close.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Link label, Link, Open first question, Add FAQ rich results data, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Question.

Blog posts

Your latest blog articles.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Blog, Link label, Posts, Columns on desktop, Show excerpt, Show date, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Image with text

A picture next to text. A simple, flexible section.

You can change: Tone, Image position, Image, Floating inset image, Image ratio, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Checklist, Button label, Button link, Link label, Link, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Rich text

A heading, text and buttons. Good for short messages.

You can change: Tone, Alignment, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Buttons.

Benefits

A row of short benefits with icons.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Heading alignment, Style, Columns on desktop, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Benefit.

Email signup

An email sign-up box.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Button label, Fine print, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Email popup

A pop-up that shows after a delay or when the mouse leaves the page.

You can change: Tone, Image, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Button label, Success message, Fine print, Show, Delay, Show again after, Campaign ID.

Product recommendations

Products related to the one being viewed.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Heading alignment, Recommendation type, Products, Visible on desktop, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Product story tabs

Tabs with the story, ingredients and how to use a product.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Tabs accessible name, Layout, Side image, Alignment, Ingredients metafield, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Tab, Ingredient.

Contact form

Contact details and a contact form.

You can change: Tone, Eyebrow, Heading, Text, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: Contact detail.

Collection banner

The banner at the top of a collection page.

You can change: Tone, Style, Image, Image overlay, Show breadcrumbs, Show description, Show product count, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Apps

A place to add app blocks.

You can change: Constrain to page width, Tone, Top padding, Bottom padding.

Blocks you can add: App block.

Custom Liquid

For developers: your own code.

You can change: Liquid, Constrain to page width, Tone, Top padding, Bottom padding.
