Changelog

What changed in each version.

1.7.1

  • New Photo shape setting for the product page, featured product, product story and collection banner.
  • Quick buy now has a thumbnail strip with arrows and a counter, like the main product page.
  • Featured product opens on the selected variant's photo.
  • Bundle block can fill itself from Shopify's complementary-product suggestions.
  • Sticky bar has a variant dropdown that stays in sync with the main picker.
  • Product story tabs scroll cleanly on phones.

1.7.0

  • New colour swatches: clear ring and tick on the chosen swatch, name tooltip, sold-out strike, and automatic text buttons when a colour is missing.
  • New Swatch colors setting to type your own shade colours.
  • Cleaner swatch dots on product cards with a +N count.
  • Documentation rewritten for beginners.

1.6.5

  • Sample press section now uses fictional publication names.

1.6.4

  • Added preset listing templates for Lumivane, Nightbloom and Fernmist.
  • Rebuilt the Image banner and Stack cards sections.
  • Switched placeholders to Shopify's built-in placeholder images.
  • Optimised image sizes and loading.
